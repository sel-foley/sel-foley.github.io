Model results

Each card is one silent video. Lanes are the human-curated target stem and each system's output for the text query. Press a lane to hear it with the video from the start; one lane plays at a time.

StemFoley-3.3K evaluation clips

10 videos

Ten multi-source clips from the StemFoley-3.3K evaluation data, selected for this page. Their parent videos never appear in training. Each video has two or three target captions: pick a text query above the video, and every lane switches to that query. The target stem is the source isolated from the recording, a reference rather than a generated output.

Human-evaluation items

15 videos

These fifteen items were used in our blinded human evaluation. They are independently curated UnAV videos outside StemFoley-3.3K, excluded from every dataset split. System identities were hidden and the presentation order was randomized during the study; they are revealed here. SelFoley+SyncAdapt was not part of this evaluation.
