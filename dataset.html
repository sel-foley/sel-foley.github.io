StemFoley-3.3K

A human-curated multitrack dataset for selective video-to-audio: 3,357 videos with isolated source stems and source-level captions. Every clip comes with its original mix, one stem per source, and a caption and active intervals for each stem. Fifteen reviewers listened to every stem.

How it was built

Candidates are proposed automatically and then audited by people. Nothing reaches the dataset without a human having listened to it.

Reasoner proposalA vision-language reasoner lists the sound sources of each clip and drafts a caption for every source.
Candidate stem bankA text-promptable audio separation model extracts one stem candidate per proposed source.
Full listening by 15 reviewersEvery candidate stem is listened to against its caption and the original mix; unclear or leaking stems are rejected.
Caption cleanupCaptions are corrected to describe exactly what the stem contains, concisely.
Sibling overlap re-auditStems of the same clip are compared against each other once more so that sibling sources stay distinct.

Stems are a subset of the mix, not a decomposition of it: background sound that belongs to no proposed source stays in the original mix only.

Examples

40 clips · 93 stems

StemFoley-3.3K pairs real-world videos with human-verified, source-separated stems and source-level captions. These forty clips illustrate the source diversity and separation granularity the dataset provides: ambience and action, human and non-human sources, realistic and animated footage, transient and continuous sounds. The first ten each have three sources; on the others, shaded bands mark where each source is active.

Each card shows the video, the original mix, and every stem with its caption. Press a lane to hear it with the video from the start; one lane plays at a time. These are stems separated from the original recordings, not model outputs. Generated audio is on the results page.

Release

StemFoley-3.3K, the SelFoley checkpoint and the evaluation code will be released upon acceptance under the applicable source licenses. Before release, source licenses are documented, identifying speech is removed where the source terms require it, and only media that may be redistributed is published.
